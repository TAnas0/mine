# Tracking Global Macro Alternatives

As the global financial landscape shifts toward multi-polarity, institutional reserve assets like the IMF's SDR are being compared against new alternative concepts like the BRICS Unit and traditional hedges like Gold.

The dashboard below provides a **professional macro-economic comparison** over a 5-year window. All assets are normalized to a baseline of 100 at the start of the period to show relative performance.

<MacroTracker client:load />

## Why this comparison?

This view allows us to analyze how different "units of account" respond to global economic shifts:

1. **SDR (IMF Standard)**: A basket of major global currencies (USD, EUR, CNY, JPY, GBP). It represents the established institutional reserve asset.
2. **BRICS Unit (Alternative)**: A conceptual asset combining 40% Gold and 60% of the BRICS currency basket (BRL, RUB, INR, CNY, ZAR). It aims to offer lower correlation to USD volatility.
3. **Gold (The Anchor)**: The world's oldest reserve asset, providing an intrinsic value floor.

*Note: This is a conceptual financial model and does not constitute financial advice. The data shown is for demonstrative purposes.*
